# Dashboard Layout

Header: OLIST — Análise de Vendas e Entregas.
Four white cards: Faturamento Total, Volume de Pedidos, Valor Total com Frete, Tempo Médio de Entrega.
First chart row: Top 10 categories, orders by status (donut), average delivery time by state (horizontal bars).
Second chart row: revenue by state (horizontal bars), monthly revenue (line), Top 10 cities grouped by city and state (table).
Light background, navy primary color, consistent spacing and no additional KPI measures. Slicers are optional and are not added in this version.
